Key findings

  • Gwanda runs the highest loss ratio at 75%; Gweru the lowest at 69%.
  • Theft claims carry the highest suspected-fraud rate (16.1%), versus 3.3% for Fire.
  • Claims reported more than 6 days after the loss are flagged 1.8x as often as prompt ones.
  • Suspected-fraud claims account for $2,485,750 of paid losses (13.1%) - the leakage target for tighter triage.

Data note: dataset is synthetic, modelled on a typical vehicle-insurance claims schema, built for portfolio demonstration. Loss ratio = claims paid ÷ earned premium (shown when no cause filter is applied). Fraud = flagged as suspected, not confirmed.
